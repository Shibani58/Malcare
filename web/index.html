Ask about malaria or tuberculosis

Hybrid retrieval (BM25 + MiniLM sentence embeddings) finds the most relevant passages, and a DistilBERT reader extracts the exact answer. It only quotes its sources, so it cannot make up medical facts.

The language models load when you start asking (about 90 MB, cached after the first visit).

How it works

Image classifiers

Two transfer-learning models: a MobileNet feature extractor followed by a 100-unit dense layer and a 2-class softmax (uninfected vs. infected). Images are centre-cropped to 224×224 and scaled to [-1, 1].

In-browser inference

The original Keras models are converted to TensorFlow.js at deploy time, so predictions run locally on your device with no server and no data upload.

Question answering

Articles are split into overlapping passages. BM25 and MiniLM embeddings each rank them, Reciprocal Rank Fusion merges the two rankings, and a DistilBERT model fine-tuned on SQuAD extracts the answer span, all with Transformers.js.
